- **What changed.** The module (330 lines on main, over the 400-line limit once typed) is split by responsibility, each module with its place in `crc/oci/spot_vm/README.md`: `client.py` (typed Protocols `ComputeClient` / `BlockClient` of the six OCI calls used, `RegionClients`, factories on top of `Base`), `decide.py` (state, preemptible, OKE, tag and age checks through `crc.core.policy` and `crc.core.age`), `leftovers.py` (attachment discovery, wait for `TERMINATED`, volume tag check and delete), `cleaner.py` (`SpotVmCleaner`: read-only `plan()`, mutating `apply()`, summary log, registry factory) and `adapter.py` (`SpotVM`). `crc.oci.spot_vm` still exports `SpotVM` (and `oci`, `Base`, which the existing tests patch). `SpotVM` keeps its constructor (plus an optional trailing `runtime: SpotVmRuntime | None`), attributes (`instance_names_to_delete`, `volumes_to_delete`, `dry_run`, `filter_tags`, `exception_tags`, `age`, `custom_age_tag_key`, `notags`, `default_instance_state`, the two wait constants), `get_deleted`, `delete_count`, `get_deleted_volumes`, `delete()` and the private hooks the legacy tests call (`_is_preemptible`, `_is_managed_or_oke_node`, `_has_matching_filter_tags`, `_attached_block_volume_ids`, `_wait_instance_terminated`, `_volume_ok_to_delete`, `_delete_volume`); it still subclasses `Service` and `Base` and `crc.py` is unchanged. Per-instance errors are logged and isolated exactly as on main.
